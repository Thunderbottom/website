Posts are Markdown (MDX). The headings you write become the table of contents.

## A section

Text, **bold**, _italics_, `inline code`, [links](https://example.com), lists and
quotes all work.

### A subsection

Sub-headings nest under their section in the table of contents.

## Sidenotes

A sidenote sits in the margin on wide screens and opens on tap on a phone.<Sidenote id="1">The text of the note. It can have [a link](https://example.com) too.</Sidenote> Give each one a number that is unique within the post.
